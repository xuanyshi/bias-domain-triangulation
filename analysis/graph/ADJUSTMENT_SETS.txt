DAG and adjustment sets

The DAG contains 19 nodes (X, Y and C01-C17) and 132 directed edges.
The target is the total effect of prenatal acetaminophen use (X) on the specified
recorded offspring ASD or ADHD outcome (Y), interpreted separately by outcome.

Set A: C01, C02, C03, C04, C05, C06, C07, C10, C11.
Set B: C01, C02, C03, C04, C05, C06, C07, C09, C11, C12, C13.
Overall Strong requires complete coverage of either set in the same model,
outcome and exposure window. Set B requires a qualifying pre-exposure C13
component. B1-B3 retain set A as their domain reference.

DAGitty 0.3.4 enumerated both sets. Independent d-separation and single-member
deletion checks confirmed sufficiency and minimality for the supplied graph.
These graph properties depend on the stated causal assumptions and construct
boundaries; representative measurements may incompletely capture a construct.

Edge integration
Of 342 assessed directions, 135 received Pass. Omitting C12 -> C08,
C14 -> C15 and C17 -> C13 resolved ten directed cycles, yielding 132 edges.
The three original Pass decisions are recorded separately from DAG inclusion.
X -> Y represents the focal research hypothesis.
